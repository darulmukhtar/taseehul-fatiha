Terms

Terms, refunds & privacy

Places and payment

  • The course fee is £3 for all four sessions, paid by PayPal.
  • Places are limited to 10 students. Registration closes when all places are taken, or when the course begins on Friday 27 November 2026 at 7:00pm UK time.
  • Your place is confirmed only after your payment has been received and checked. We will email you to confirm.

Cancellation and refunds

  • You may cancel at any time by emailing ayazhassaan@gmail.com.
  • A full refund is available only if you cancel before the course starts (Friday 27 November 2026, 7:00pm UK time).
  • No refunds are available once the course has started.

Your information

  • We collect your name, email address, optional PayPal name and, if you choose, your WhatsApp number, only to run this course.
  • Your details are stored in a private spreadsheet that only the course organiser can access. They are not shown on this website and are never sold or shared.
  • Your WhatsApp number is used only to invite you to the course group, and only if you ticked the box to agree.
  • We will delete your details once the course has finished. To ask for them to be deleted sooner, email ayazhassaan@gmail.com.
